Know the difference

Plywood or block board?

Plywood

Layered for all-round strength

Thin hardwood veneers glued at right angles. Strong in every direction, holds screws firmly and is the first choice for carcasses, kitchens and wardrobes.

See plywood Block board

Solid core for long spans

Solid timber strips between veneers. Lighter and very stiff along its length — ideal for doors, long shelves, beds and table tops that must not sag.

What is BWP plywood, and where should I use it?
BWP stands for Boiling Water Proof. The layers are bonded with a waterproof resin, so the board copes with moisture far better than ordinary plywood. Use it for kitchens, bathroom vanities, wash areas and anywhere near water. All Liveteck plywood is BWP grade.
What does “100% Gurjan face veneer” mean?
Gurjan is a dense, strong hardwood. Our plywood, block boards, flexi plywood and flush doors are faced with 100% Gurjan veneer, which gives a smooth, even surface that takes laminate, veneer, polish or paint well.
Which plywood thickness is best for kitchens and wardrobes?
Carcasses and shutters are usually made in 19 mm or 16 mm plywood. Back panels and drawer bottoms use 6 mm or 9 mm, and partitions or panelling 12 mm. Tell us your design and we will suggest the right mix.
