Which plywood thickness should I use for wardrobes?
Wardrobe carcasses are usually built in 19 mm or 16 mm plywood, with 6 mm or 9 mm for back panels. Tell us the design and we will suggest the right combination.
What is the difference between plywood and block board?
Plywood is made of thin cross-laminated veneers and is strong in every direction. Block board has a solid timber-strip core, making it lighter and stiff over long spans — great for doors and long shelves.
