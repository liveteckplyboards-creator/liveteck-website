Inside every sheet

Engineered layer
by layer.

01 / 05

Selected face veneer

A smooth, even hardwood face — the surface you see, ready for laminate, veneer, polish or paint.

02 / 05

Cross-laminated plies

Each layer is laid at 90° to the next, so the board resists warping, splitting and twisting.

03 / 05

Dense hardwood core

Solid hardwood plies at the heart of the sheet give strength, stiffness and firm screw holding.

04 / 05

Bonded under pressure

Layers are glued and pressed under heat and pressure into one solid, stable panel.

05 / 05

Calibrated & finished

Sanded to an even thickness so every sheet sits flat, joins cleanly and finishes beautifully.
