Liveteck Ply & Boards is a Kakinada-based plywood and interior building-materials business, serving homeowners and professionals since 2015.

Our story

Rooted in Kakinada.
Built on quality.

Liveteck Ply & Boards was established in 2015 by Amith Jain on Temple Street, Kakinada, with a simple aim — to give the people who build homes and interiors materials they can genuinely rely on.

Today we offer a complete range of plywood, block boards, flexi plywood, veneers, laminates and flush doors for residential, commercial and interior applications — from the carcass of a wardrobe to the final decorative surface.

Homeowners, interior designers, architects, contractors, builders, carpenters and furniture manufacturers come to us for dependable products, honest guidance and service that puts the customer first.

  • Recommending boards for kitchens and other moisture-prone areas
  • Pairing laminates and veneers with the look you want
  • Flexi plywood solutions for curves, columns and counters
  • Estimating quantities for homes, interiors and larger projects
